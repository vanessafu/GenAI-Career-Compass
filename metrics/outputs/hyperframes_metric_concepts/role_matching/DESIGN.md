# CareerCompass Metric Concept: Role Matching

## Style Prompt
Clean modern presentation visual for a fixed-fixture role-matching quality metric. The image should help a slide team understand hierarchy: nDCG@9 and bucket accuracy are decision metrics, Precision@3 and MRR@9 are smoke checks, and guardrails are reliability checks.

## Colors
- Background: #f8f5ee
- Ink: #151821
- Muted text: #667085
- Primary accent: #0f766e
- Secondary accent: #2458d8
- Warning accent: #c98218
- Risk accent: #b42318
- Soft line: #ded8ca

## Typography
- Display and body: Aptos / Segoe UI fallback
- Data: Cascadia Mono / Consolas fallback

## What NOT to Do
- No generated or inferred numbers.
- No dense dashboard chrome.
- No stock imagery.
- No unlabeled heatmap cells.
